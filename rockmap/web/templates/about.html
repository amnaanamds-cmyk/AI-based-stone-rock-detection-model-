About RockMap

RockMap v{{ version }} — AI-Based Satellite Rock/Stone Detection and Geological Mapping System — Final Year Project, Department of Computer Science, Government Degree College Zaim, Charsadda (Bacha Khan University Charsadda). By Aleena (Roll No 40) and Amna (Roll No 39).

Processing pipeline

  1. Acquisition — Sentinel-2 L2A scenes and the Copernicus 30 m DEM are read directly from AWS Open Data; the least-cloudy late-summer scenes of every 20 km tile are merged into a cloud-free median composite.
  2. Pre-processing — DN → surface reflectance, cloud / shadow masking (SCL, QA_PIXEL or Haze Optimized Transform), topographic C-correction for steep slopes, band stacking and DEM co-registration.
  3. Surface masks — snow / glacier, water, dense vegetation and deep terrain shadow are mapped as separate classes so they are never mistaken for rock.
  4. Feature extraction — 6 reflectance bands, 7 spectral indices (NDVI, clay SWIR1/SWIR2, iron-oxide red/blue, ferrous SWIR1/NIR, ferric red/green, brightness, bare-rock index) and 6 terrain features (elevation, slope, aspect sin/cos, hillshade, roughness).
  5. Training data — stratified pixel samples from a reference geological map, split into spatial blocks (train / validation / test) to avoid spatial autocorrelation leakage.
  6. Models — a fully convolutional CNN (9×9 receptive field) benchmarked against Random Forest and SVM.
  7. Map generation — seamless tile-by-tile classification, majority-filter smoothing, region-wide GeoTIFF mosaics, web map tiles, GeoJSON polygons, district statistics and PDF reports.
  8. Validation — overall accuracy, Cohen's kappa, per-class precision / recall / F1 and confusion matrix against the reference map.
