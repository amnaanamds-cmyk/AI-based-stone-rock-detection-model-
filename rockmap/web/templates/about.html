About RockMap

AI-Based Satellite Rock/Stone Detection and Geological Mapping System — Final Year Project, Department of Computer Science, Government Degree College Zaim, Charsadda (Bacha Khan University Charsadda). By Aleena (Roll No 40) and Amna (Roll No 39).

Processing pipeline

  1. Pre-processing — DN → surface reflectance, optional dark-object subtraction, cloud / shadow masking (SCL, QA_PIXEL or Haze Optimized Transform), band stacking and DEM co-registration.
  2. Feature extraction — 6 reflectance bands, 7 spectral indices (NDVI, clay SWIR1/SWIR2, iron-oxide red/blue, ferrous SWIR1/NIR, ferric red/green, brightness, bare-rock index) and 6 terrain features (elevation, slope, aspect sin/cos, hillshade, roughness).
  3. Training data — stratified pixel samples from a reference geological map, split into spatial blocks (train / validation / test) to avoid spatial autocorrelation leakage.
  4. Models — a fully convolutional CNN (9×9 receptive field) benchmarked against Random Forest and SVM.
  5. Map generation — dense per-pixel classification, majority-filter smoothing, colour-coded GeoTIFF / PNG maps and area statistics.
  6. Validation — overall accuracy, Cohen's kappa, per-class precision / recall / F1 and confusion matrix against the reference map.
